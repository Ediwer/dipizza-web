DiPizza · Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 3, 2026. When you send a catering enquiry, DiPizza uses the information you provide to respond, prepare a quote, and manage a possible booking.

Information you may provide

This can include your name, company, email address, phone number, event type and date, guest count, venue, and any details you choose to include. Please do not include payment card details or sensitive personal information in the message.

How your enquiry is handled

Enquiry details are stored in DiPizza’s Supabase project so authorized team members can review and manage requests. The details are also emailed to dipizza00@gmail.com, with a copy to ediwergraterol@gmail.com. FormSubmit handles those notification emails and sends an acknowledgement to the email address you provide. The website is hosted on Vercel and delivered through Cloudflare; those providers may process technical request data to serve and secure the site. Supabase stores the enquiry, and FormSubmit processes the email delivery.

The enquiry form does not collect payment information. Information is used for responding to and managing your catering enquiry and any resulting booking; it is not used for unrelated promotional email.

Spam prevention and security

To limit automated abuse, the form uses a signed challenge and rate limits. A one-way hash derived from the connection IP, the time, and the result of an attempt is retained for up to seven days. The original IP address is not stored in that abuse record. The browser’s user agent is stored with the enquiry to help identify automated submissions. Access to enquiry and booking records is restricted to authorized administrators.

Retention and your choices

DiPizza keeps enquiry information while it is needed to respond, manage a booking, and meet applicable recordkeeping or dispute-resolution needs. You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to any records DiPizza must retain. Contact dipizza00@gmail.com.
